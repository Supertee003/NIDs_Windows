## 3. What this proves

1. **The boot-ring fix holds.** 30.8 h with `minifilter_ring_dropped=0` and
   `pipeline_dropped=0`, including the boot that previously lost 448 and 4,012
   rows. The daemon-first / 1 s retry design is stable over a long window.
2. **The stack is stable.** No crash, no SCM restart, no worker failure for
   30.8 h. This is the strongest #30 evidence so far (the stale FIM helper fix
   is deployed and held).
3. **The remaining loss is one lane.** All queue drops and all policy shed are
   `capture_etw`. Nose, minifilter, registry sensor, host_net and WFP are clean.
4. **The zero-loss gate is still not met.** 1,395 rows overflowed the ETW share
   (1,394 of them registry changes, the signal-bearing class the margin was
   meant to protect), and 446,327 low-value rows were deliberately shed.

## 4. Why the registry drops still happen

`ETW_SHARE_MARGIN = 256` reserves the last 256 slots of the ETW share for the
non-sheddable classes. The host produced 11.17 M registry events in 30.8 h
(~100/s average) and the bursts exceed that 256-slot reserve, so 1,394 registry
rows were refused by the same quota the margin was protecting. The margin is
too small for this registry volume; it is not a boot or minifilter issue.

Options:

| Option | Work | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Declare a loss budget** | owner decision + record + confirmation window | closes with a measured, bounded, attributable loss; no code change |
| **B. Raise the ETW reserve** (256 -> 1,024 or 2,048) | source + E2 + deploy + 24 h | drives registry queue drops toward 0; sheds more low-value writes; still a policy-shed budget |
| **C. Dynamic share** (lend unused FIM share to ETW) | larger queue-policy change | attacks the cause (static 50/50 split) but needs a new fair-share design and E5 |
| **D. Registry coalescing** | detection-semantics change | cuts the 11 M volume at the source; highest risk to persistence detection |

Recommendation: **A for the release candidate**, with **B** as the next
candidate if the owner wants zero overflow rather than a declared budget.
A is the only option that closes now without another 24 h window, and the loss
is already counted and attributable rather than silent.

## 5. Budget if A is chosen
