## Claim

The HEAD nose binary emits v3 frames from real Npcap capture on the lab adapter,
and the HEAD daemon decodes them through the strict v3 validation path
(exact `165 + payload_len` length, `reserved == 0`, CRC32 over the attached
payload, provenance-vs-flags consistency) with zero rejected frames — while the
previously installed (v3-unaware) daemon drops exactly those frames.

## Method

Rollout ran in the dependency order the slice requires: **new producer against
the old consumer first (negative control), then the daemon swap while the new
producer kept running.**

Artifacts are in `admin-evidence/wire-v3-e5-20261109/`:
`health-baseline.json`, `health-phaseA.json`, `health-phaseB.json`,
`health-phaseC.json`, `nose-phaseA.log`, `rollout.log`, `e5_rollout.ps1`.

Adapter: `\Device\NPF_{CE375996-106D-46F7-A67C-0168EAE8C96F}` (VMnet1, 192.168.126.1),
bound with `-iface ... -require-iface`. Traffic: ICMP echo plus TCP connect/write
probes to the lab guest 192.168.126.10.

## Limitations (do not overclaim)

1. **No independent CRC recomputation of live frames.** An out-of-process pipe tap
   was attempted (`pipe_tap_v3.py`) so a non-product decoder could re-verify every
   frame; it failed because the pywin32 pipe instance could not accept the nose Go
   client's `GENERIC_WRITE` open (first attempt) and the second attempt hit
   "all pipe instances are busy" from the orphaned first instance. The strict
   decoder's own `nose_frames_rejected == 0` over 67 frames is the proxy, not a
   proof by an independent implementation.
2. **Payload bytes in the forensic ring were not read back.** The control surface
   has no per-record inspection (`forensics.show` answers
   `control_daemon_query_failed_or_not_implemented`), so "payload reached the ring"
   rests on `submitted` counting frames that carry payload, not on inspecting a record.
3. **Sparse IPv4 only.** 67 frames of ICMP/TCP probe traffic; no throughput test, no
   IPv6 (v3 v6-tail path) coverage, no truncated-frame boundary probe on the wire.
4. **Not E6/E7.** Single host, single adapter, no release packaging.

## Operational facts recorded by this run

- **Restart cost is real and measurable:** 31 frames lost in a 14-second service
  window. The daemon-first order keeps this bounded; the reverse order would have
  dropped every v3 frame.
- **Config drift:** `HKLM\AEGIS_NOSE_IFACE` is
  `\Device\NPF_{5FD31C6E-DE48-44CB-838E-14667A04DA4B}` (Killer Wi-Fi AX1650i), not the
  VMnet1 lab adapter `{CE375996-…}`. This run passed `-iface` explicitly and left
  the machine variable untouched.
- **No nose supervisor:** no service and no scheduled task owns `aegis-nose.exe`.
  After a reboot nothing starts packet acquisition. Live state at the end of this
  run: daemon pid 24804 (HEAD build) + nose pid 18968 on VMnet1 (v3 producer).

## Remaining risk

- Rollback restores a v3-unaware daemon; the VMnet1 nose must then be stopped or
  replaced with a pre-v3 producer in the same action, otherwise every frame is
  dropped again (measured: +24 frames per 19 s at this traffic rate).
- The v3 evidence still has no throughput/back-pressure measurement at line rate.
