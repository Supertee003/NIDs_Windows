1. The loss counters are cumulative for the process generation. They cannot
   decrease. Restarting the daemon or minifilter changes
   `process_generation_ms`, which `host_soak_check.py:236` scores as a void.
2. `--hours` is an owner-approved acceptance criterion: `final_audit.py:335`
   requires `required_hours >= 24`. Lowering it, lowering the budgets, editing
   `host_soak_check.py`, or writing a PASS artifact are policy bypass /
   forensic inconsistency and are forbidden (AGENTS.md STOP-THE-LINE).
3. A 12 h soak cannot close `soak_24h` for the same reason.
