# ADR-0012 decision — create-once measurement on the NIDS host (E5, 2026-11-17)

**Gap:** `ADR-0012-DUP-RATIO-E5-20261108` (result `MEASURED_IDENTITY_UNVERIFIED`)
**ADR:** `docs/adr/0012-fim-ingress-volume.md`
**Host:** this Windows NIDS host (victim = this host; single-host test model)
**Installed runtime:** `aegis_nids.exe` `7955A6E3...`, daemon PID 23072
**Probe:** live `fim_probe` counters from `tools/aegisctl.py queue --json`
**Result:** `NO_DUPLICATE_DELIVERY` → **reject a suppressing coalescer**

---

## 1. Question

The probe reports same-path/same-PID repeats inside one pull batch (47.5% of
2.4M minifilter records on 2026-11-17). The open question was whether those
repeats are **duplicate delivery of one open** (safe to coalesce) or **distinct
opens of the same path** (not safe to coalesce).

The 2026-11-08 measurement could not answer it: `KeQuerySystemTime` has ~15.6 ms
granularity, so distinct opens share a timestamp, and the coarse key aggregates
them. The gap closed only when the identity question was answered by an
experiment whose ground truth is known before the measurement.

## 2. Method

`admin-evidence/adr0012-single-host-20261117/pilot_create_once.py`

1. Sample `fim_probe.minifilter` counters, idle 30 s, sample again → ambient
   `duplicates_per_pid` rate per second.
2. Create **N files with unique names, exactly one open each** (`open(..., "xb")`,
   one write, close) inside `D:\tmp\aegis-adr0012-<ts>\`.
3. Sample counters immediately after the churn.
4. Subtract the ambient rate scaled to the churn duration and compare the
   excess with N.

Ground truth: if the delivery layer duplicated a create, a unique path opened
once would appear twice, giving `Δduplicates_per_pid ≈ N`. If the layer
delivers once, the excess is ambient noise only.

## 3. Results

| Run | Files | Idle | Churn | Δ minifilter records | Δ duplicates_per_pid | Ambient expected | Excess | Ratio to files |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pilot | 1,000 | 30.182 s | 0.304 s | 1,015 | 6 | 0.7 | 5.3 | 0.0053 |
| scale | 20,000 | 30.186 s | 5.822 s | 20,042 | 12 | 11.8 | 0.2 | 0.00001 |

Both runs: `minifilter.dropped=0`, `minifilter.pipeline_dropped=0`,
`dropped_total=0`, `shed_overload=0`, `batch_overflow=0`.

In the 20,000-file run, 20,042 minifilter records arrived for 20,000 single-open
creates and 12 repeats were counted — statistically indistinguishable from the
11.8 expected from ambient host activity.

## 4. Inference

1. A create-once workload produces **no repeat rows** at the delivery layer.
2. Therefore the 47.5% production repeat ratio is not duplicate delivery; the
   rows that repeat a `(path, pid)` inside a batch are separate successful
   opens (open/write/close cycles by the same process — exactly what a real
   workload does).
3. Suppressing those rows would discard distinct observations, so the proposed
   minifilter coalescer must not deploy.
4. `duplicates_same_ts` is confirmed as an unusable signal: the create-once
   runs reported 0 same-timestamp repeats while production reports 703,873.

## 5. Decision

- **Reject the minifilter `(path, kind, pid)` coalescer.** It would drop
  distinct opens and violate ADR-0010's "loss must be visible" bar.
- Close `ADR-0012-DUP-RATIO-E5-20261108` at **E5** with this measurement.
- `GAP-QLOSS-ISOLATION-002` stays **open**: the FIM volume is real, so the
  lever is the ETW Kernel-File `NameDelete` lane (18,151,604 rows, the actual
  volume leader) and consumer throughput — not FIM coalescing.
- The native poll `(path, kind)` idea in the ADR remains unproven and unused;
  this measurement does not cover it.

## 6. Limits (do not overclaim)

- Covered: **create-once** opens on NTFS on this host with the installed daemon.
- Not covered: rewrite/recreate loops, the `ReadDirectoryChangesW` poll lane,
  network drives, other machines, other filters.
- The result does not prove that every production repeat is a *meaningful*
  open; it proves the delivery layer does not duplicate, which is the specific
  gate the ADR required.
- No daemon restart, no source change, no re-sign; the installed image and the
  signed release tree are untouched.
